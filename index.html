Questions about hiring movers

Who does the moving?

Students from UNC Wilmington, each one trained by founder Jared Mazzola. The same crew handles your job from start to finish.

How do I get a price?

Call (704) 999-3714 or send the estimate form. We ask about both addresses, the number of bedrooms, stairs and any heavy items, then give you a free estimate. We reply the same day during business hours.

Do I need to rent the truck?

For loading and unloading help, most customers book their own truck or container and we load and unload it. Not sure what to book? See the truck size guide or call and we will help you work it out.

Is there a minimum?

Yes. The minimum booking is two movers for two hours. The estimate is free and there is no deposit to book.

Can you come today?

Yes, we take same-day jobs. Call (704) 999-3714 and we will tell you when a crew can get to you.
