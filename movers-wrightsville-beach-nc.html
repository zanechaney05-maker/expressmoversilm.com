Good to know about moving in Wrightsville Beach

The drawbridge opens for boats

When the Heide Trask Drawbridge goes up, traffic onto the island stops. Leave a little slack in your timing.

Parking is metered most of the year

The town meters its parking from March 1 to October 31, and spaces fill up in summer. Line up a spot for the truck before move day.

Tell us about the stairs

Many beach houses and condos sit a floor or more above the ground. Tell us how many flights there are and whether there is an elevator.
