Good to know about moving in Leland

The bridges back up

The bridges between Wilmington and Leland slow down at rush hour. A mid-morning start avoids the worst of it.

Check your community's rules

Planned communities such as Brunswick Forest, Waterford and Magnolia Greens can have their own rules for moving trucks. Ask your HOA or gatehouse before move day.

If you are moving in with a rental truck or a portable container, we unload it, carry everything to the right rooms and put the furniture back together.
