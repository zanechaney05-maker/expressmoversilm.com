Piano, gun safe, hot tub and pool table movers in Wilmington, NC

Some things take more than two people and a dolly. We bring extra hands for the heavy and awkward ones, on their own or as part of a move.

Heavy items we move

We move four kinds of heavy item. Each one needs extra hands and extra care, so each carries an added fee.

Gun safe moving

Tell us the make or the rough weight, and whether it has to go up or down any steps.

Upright piano moving

We move uprights only, not grand pianos. Tell us about stairs and tight turns at both ends.

Hot tub moving

Tell us the size, whether it is drained and disconnected, and how we reach it: gates, decks and steps.

Pool table moving

Tell us the size of the table and which floor it is on.

Two real jobs: a pool table and an upright piano.

What heavy item moving costs

Heavy items carry an added fee that depends on the item. Tell us what it is, where it sits now and where it is going, and we will give you a price. The estimate is free and there is no deposit to book.

Questions about heavy items

Which heavy items do you move?

Gun safes, upright pianos, hot tubs and pool tables.

Do you move grand pianos?

No. We move upright pianos only.

Can I book you for one heavy item, without a full move?

Yes. Pick "A heavy item moved" on the estimate form, or call (704) 999-3714.

Why is there an added fee?

These items need extra hands and extra care. The fee depends on the item, so tell us about it when you ask for your estimate.

How far will you travel?

Up to about an hour and ten minutes from Wilmington. Jobs outside Wilmington may carry a travel fee. See the service area.

Got something heavy?

Tell us what it is and where it is going. We reply the same day during business hours.
