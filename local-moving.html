Getting ready for move day

We charge by the hour, so a little preparation keeps the bill down.

What we need from you

  • Everything boxed and taped before we arrive. We do not pack or supply boxes.
  • Washers, dryers and fridges disconnected.
  • Moving blankets, if you want furniture wrapped.
  • A heads-up about anything heavy: safes, pianos, hot tubs, pool tables.

Tips that save time

  • Write the room on every box, so it goes to the right place the first time.
  • Clear a path from the door to where the truck will park.
  • If your building has an elevator or loading area to reserve, book it for move day.
  • Keep keys, medicine and important papers with you, not in a box.
