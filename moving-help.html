Moving help in Wilmington, NC: loading and unloading

Hourly moving labor for the truck, trailer, storage unit or portable container you rent. We bring the crew to load it, unload it, or both. Book just one end if that is all you need.

What our moving labor includes

  • A load only, an unload only, or both ends of the trip
  • Carrying everything from your home to the truck or container
  • Loading and stacking so the load stays put on the road
  • Unloading at the other end and placing things in the right rooms
  • Rental trucks, trailers, storage units and portable containers such as PODS
  • Hand trucks, dollies with straps and hand tools, at no extra charge
  • Wrapping your furniture in your moving blankets, at no extra charge

What to have ready

  • Moving blankets and tie-down straps, if you want furniture wrapped and secured. Most truck rental counters offer them.
  • Boxes packed and taped before we arrive. We do not pack.
  • Washers, dryers and fridges disconnected.

What moving help costs

We charge by the hour for the crew, with a minimum of two movers for two hours. The total depends on how much there is to carry, stairs and long walks to the truck.

Call (704) 999-3714 or send the form and we will quote your job. The estimate is free and there is no deposit to book.

Questions about moving help

Do I need to rent the truck?

Most customers book their own truck or container and we load and unload it. Not sure what to book? Call and we will help you work it out.

Can I book just a load or just an unload?

Yes. Some jobs are one end only. Book us to load, to unload, or for both.

Can you come today?

Yes, we take same-day jobs. Call (704) 999-3714 and we will tell you when a crew can get to you.

Do you bring equipment?

Yes. Hand trucks, dollies with straps and hand tools come with every job at no extra charge. Have moving blankets and tie-down straps on hand if you want furniture wrapped and secured in the truck.

Do you wrap furniture?

Yes, at no extra charge. Have moving blankets on hand and we wrap your furniture in them before it goes on the truck.

Can you load a PODS container or a storage unit?

Yes. We load and unload portable storage containers, such as PODS, and storage units, as well as rental trucks and trailers.

How far will you travel?

Up to about an hour and ten minutes from Wilmington. Jobs outside Wilmington may carry a travel fee. See the service area.

How do I book and pay?

Call or text (704) 999-3714, or send the estimate form. There is no deposit to book, and you can pay by cash, Venmo, or invoice.

Need a crew for your truck?

Tell us the date, both addresses and the size of the truck or container. We reply the same day during business hours.
